# Question Bank Verification Report

Generated by `scripts/verify_question_bank.py` (deterministic: exact integer / Fraction / Decimal arithmetic, exact string comparison, closed reference tables; no sampling and no model judgement). Re-run: `python3 -I scripts/verify_question_bank.py --out docs/qa`.

## 1. Verdict

- **Items checked:** 3032 (`content-pack-v1.json`: 2472 numeric/visual-math items + 560 spatial items). **Assertions evaluated:** 67139.
- **Not every question is correct.** 129 items (4%) have an answer-key or question-validity defect (tier A), and 333 more are unanswerable or give the answer away on screen (tier B, spatial formats only).
- **The core arithmetic bank is sound.** Of 2472 non-spatial items, every answer key was recomputed independently and 2465 are correct; the 7 defective ones are all `dataPlot` (3 ties for 'most', 4 impossible 'how many more' comparisons).
- **The spatial bank (560 items, all live in the app) is where the problems are.** Only `solidAttributes` (80) is fully clean. Details below.
- A separate audio defect: 35 clips are missing, and 966 clips have no generation record, so their text cannot be proven current.

## 4. What this report does not cover

- **Hints and lesson text** (`hints`, `lesson-plans-k5.json`) were not checked.
- **Spoken audio content.** Whether an MP3 actually says its text needs a transcript; that is not deterministic and was not attempted. Only file existence and, where a generation record exists, text equality were checked.
- **Visual rendering of the iOS views** was inferred from the Swift source (which payload fields are read), not from running the app.
- Reference tables (shape facts, rotational symmetry, solid clues, piece-set geometry, net folding) are small closed sets printed at the top of the script; they are the only external knowledge used and are open to review.
- `symmetryMirror` correctness cannot be verified independently: its options contain only a boolean flag.
